S7 privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who uses S7

S7 is a personal assistant run by its owner, for the owner only. No one else can sign in to it or use it.

Google data S7 accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads events, and creates events when the owner asks.
  • Gmail: reads messages. Access is read-only: S7 never sends, changes or deletes mail.

How the data is used

Only to answer the owner's own requests, and to send the owner reminders and a morning summary of their day.

Where the data goes

S7 runs on the owner's own devices. To answer a request, the relevant text is sent to the AI model service S7 uses, and the answer goes to the owner's own Telegram chat. The data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Storage and removal

Anything S7 keeps stays on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own backups. The owner can remove S7's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

S7's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
