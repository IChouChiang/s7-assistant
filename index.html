S7

S7 is a personal assistant that one person runs for themselves. It is not a product or a service, and it isn't offered to anyone else.

With its owner's permission, S7 reads the owner's Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks, and adds calendar events when the owner asks, so it can answer questions such as "what's on tomorrow?" and send reminders. Only the owner's own Google account is ever connected.
